# Alpha 1.31: World and Exploration

## The map and the sky (1.31.1)
- **Map fog:** the map is greyed over until you have been near a place. Settings > Interface > Map fog turns it off.
- **Bought maps:** innkeepers sell "a map of these parts" for 150 gold. It clears the fog within 1.6 km and marks the places there on your map.
- **Treasure maps:**
  - Bandits, cultists and smugglers sometimes carry one, and they turn up in camps, thieves' stashes and vaults.
  - Study one (E in the inventory) to mark a spot on your map.
  - Dig there with a pickaxe or shovel. You get a buried chest, and sometimes its guardians.
- **Falling stars:** on a clear night, every few days at most, a star may fall within sight. Find the crater for Star Ore. The fall also starts a rumour in the nearest town.
- **Ash storms** in the Ashlands and deadlands choke you: a little damage and thirst (Survival mode).
- **Day and night:** the dead turn up far more often at night and rarely by day. Shy beasts like deer come out at dawn.

## Landmarks and old places (1.31.2)
All of these are laid over the world from its seed, so existing worlds and saves keep their towns and dungeons. Whatever you find goes on your map.
- **Landmarks:** a giant statue, a fallen tower, a sunken temple and a titan's bones, 14 in all. You can see them from far off, and each has a chest at its foot.
- **Under the sea:** 8 drowned ruins and sea caves, each with a chest. Swim down to them.
- **Puzzle vaults:** 9 in all. A chest sits under a stone cage, and the plaque gives a clue.
  - *Levers:* pull them in the right order. A wrong one resets them all. Lore 25 reads the clue plainly.
  - *Pillars:* turn each one to show the right sign.
  - *Mirrors:* turn them until the beam reaches the lens.
- **Ruined cities** on the far islands:
  - The dead hold them. Kill all ten and the charter stone wakes.
  - For 15,000 gold you can resettle the city. It then pays 300 gold a day, for up to 10 days at a time.
- **Roamers:** every few minutes in the wilds, something crosses the land near you.
  - Caravans are friendly.
  - Frost giants appear from level 15.
  - Dragons appear from level 30.

## Ships and climbing (1.31.3)
- **Your own ship:**
  - Innkeepers in port towns sell a Ship's Deed for 8,000 gold ("a ship of my own").
  - Use it facing deep water to launch her, and again to call her to you.
  - She sails like a boat, but much faster.
- **The open sea:**
  - Storms wear the hull and slow her.
  - Sea serpents rise beside the ship and bite the hull until you kill them, and they leave old gold.
  - A Repair Hammer patches the hull while you're aboard.
  - At 0 hull she sinks; the deed launches another.
- **Climbing:**
  - Hold Space and W against a steep rock face. It uses stamina and trains Acrobatics. Sheer built walls can't be climbed.
  - A Coil of Rope thrown up a cliff makes a line you climb like a ladder. It lasts five minutes.
