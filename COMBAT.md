# Alpha 1.27: Achievements, the Guide and Combat Depth

## Achievements and the Guide (1.27.0)
- **Achievements** (`scripts/ui/achievements.gd`), in the journal: J > Achievements.
  - There are 96, in five chapters: First Steps, Making a Name, Seasoned, Master and Beyond the Islands. They run from your first kill to level 200 on the far islands.
  - Each one says how to do it, shows your progress ("12 / 25") and links its Guide page.
  - The overview lists the next five to try, so working down the list walks you through the game.
  - **How they're tracked:**
    - Counters, fed by `Achievements.note(...)` and the quest event bus.
    - State checks: level, skills, gold, places found and cleared, quests, guilds, arena, museum, realms, the council, dragons, the far islands and more.
  - Done ones are saved with the character (WorldState `ach/<id>`). Each shows a notice the moment it's done.
- **The Guide** (`scripts/ui/guide_book.gd`): the tutorial is now a journal tab (J > Guide) instead of pop-ups.
  - There are 53 pages in 11 groups, covering every system in the game.
  - A page "comes up" the first time you need it. It gets a dot until read and a Journal Updated notice; there's no pop-up.
  - Pages you haven't come across yet wait at the bottom of the list, and you can read them any time.
  - Each page lists the achievements that go with it.
  - Settings > Interface > Tutorial notices turns the notices off.
- `Tutorial` is now a plain node: no panel, no pop-up. Old saves' hints count as read.

## Ranged (1.27.1)
- **Crossbows** for every tier:
  - One click shoots at full power, then it must be wound again (the reload).
  - They shoot **bolts**, not arrows, and pierce half of any armour.
- **Thrown weapons:** throwing knives, throwing axes and javelins for every tier.
  - Equip the stack; left click throws one, and it spins in flight.
  - They're picked up again often (85% where they land, 60% after a hit).
  - They train Archery.
- **Where it hits:**
  - A head shot (the top fifth of the body) does half again as much and shows "Head shot".
  - A leg hit (the bottom third) slows the target for 4 s.
- All of these are made at a forge. Generated by `tools/generate_alpha127.gd`.

## Gear wears down (1.27.2)
- **World Creation:** *Gear wears down* is on by default and off in the Story preset.
- **How wear works:**
  - Every hit you land wears your weapon, and every hit you take wears a piece of your armour. Smithing slows it.
  - Below 50% condition, a weapon hits softer and armour stops less, down to half at 0 (broken).
  - The inventory shows the condition.
- **Repair:**
  - A smith's **Repair** button mends everything you have on, priced by the wear.
  - A **Repair Hammer** (workbench) mends what you wear anywhere, more with Smithing.

## Builds (1.27.3 and 1.27.4)
- **Perk moves on G** (Shift+G picks the next):
  - **Shield Charge** (Guardian): rush 7 m forward; everyone in the way is struck and staggered.
  - **Whirlwind Sprint** (Wanderer): 12 m in a blink.
  - **Shadow Step** (Shadow): step behind the foe you look at and fade from sight for 3 s.
- **Talent synergies:** ten hidden bonuses, each earned by owning two perks from different trees (Spellblade, Shadow Archer, Battlemage, Juggernaut...).
- **Titles:** from level 10, your build gives you a name (the Unseen, Stormcaller...). It shows in talk, in the save list and on Level Up.
- **Loadouts:** save two builds (A and B) on Level Up, and take one up again at a temple shrine for an offering.
- **Trainer ranks:**

  | Rank | Who | Teaches up to |
  |---|---|---|
  | Journeyman | any trainer | 50 |
  | Expert | guild officers, priests | 75 |
  | Master | guild leaders | 100 |
  | Grandmaster | trainers on the far islands | 150 |

- **Skill trials:** at 50, and again at 100, a master of the skill sends word. Raise the skill three more levels and you get their amulet, made for that skill.
- **Tome of Mastery:** a rare book in the far islands' vaults. It raises your best skill of 100 or more to its next mastery step.

## Stones, styles and the saddle (1.27.5)
- **Standing stones:** every ring keeps one birthsign. Press E on the altar stone to take it as yours (once a day).
- **Fighting styles:** about one hostile person in four fights in a way that answers your best skill:
  - **Shieldbearers** take half damage from arrows that hit them from the front. Foes without a shield become **Chargers** and run at you instead.
  - **Warded** foes have 40% resistance to magic.
  - **Trackers** notice a sneak nearly twice as fast.
  - **Duelists** block, parry and dodge more.

  The style shows by the foe's name on the health bar.
- **Mounted combat:**
  - **Lances** for every tier double the gallop bonus but do 60% damage on foot.
  - **Horse barding** (iron, steel, ebon, astral) gives you armour while you ride. Use it from the inventory near your horse.
