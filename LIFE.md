# Alpha 1.30: Life Skills and Survival

## Fishing, skinning and snares (1.30.1)
- **Fishing:**
  - Fishing is a new Wilderness skill. Use a Fishing Pole (inventory or favourites) while facing water close by: a pier, a boat or a bank.
  - Wait for a bite, then press E quickly to land it. If you move, the line goes slack.
  - There are 11 fish. Which ones bite depends on the water (sea, rivers and lakes, swamps, the frozen north, the far islands) and the hour (dawn is best; some fish only bite at night).
  - Rare catches (Moonscale, Golden Koi, Gloomfin, Abyssal Angler, Leviathan Scale) are treasure. Every new kind you catch goes in the journal.
  - Common fish cook like raw fish.
- **Skinning:** F on a dead beast with a blade in your pack skins and butchers it. You get one more of everything for every 25 Hunting levels.
- **Snares** (workbench):
  - Set one on the ground outside, away from towns.
  - Every 3 hours it has a 55% chance to catch small game. E takes the catch and resets it; E on an empty snare takes it up.
  - Snares are saved where you left them.
- **Tracks:** with Hunting 15 you can see the tracks beasts leave in snow, sand and swamp mud.

## Wood, camp and water (1.30.2)
- **Woodcutting:** fallen trees in forests, highlands and the tundra give firewood and a log when chopped with an axe. A workbench splits a log into firewood.
- **Campsites:**
  - A Camping Kit (innkeepers, or a workbench) pitches camp anywhere outside, away from towns.
  - The camp has a tent, a bedroll and a fire to cook at and warm yourself by. E on the tent strikes camp.
  - Sleeping out: one night in four something finds you, unless a follower or a pet keeps watch.
- **Thirst (Survival mode):**
  - Thirst rises 5 an hour, faster in the heat, through Dry, Thirsty and Parched.
  - A Waterskin is filled by using it while facing water. Drinks and potions quench thirst a little.

## Sickness and wounds (1.30.3)
- **Disease stages:**
  - *Coming on*: half the effect, for a day.
  - *Sick*: the full effect.
  - *Severe*: twice the effect, after five days.
  - Symptoms show now and then: fever, aches, and a cough that gives you away when you sneak.
- **Cures:** an Herbal Remedy (temples, or an alchemy lab) may cure the worst disease, more often with higher Medicine. With Medicine 50, a night's sleep beats a disease that isn't severe yet.
- **Bleeding (Survival mode):** a heavy blow, or one that takes a sixth of your health, opens a wound. You can have up to three, each bleeding for 25 s, until you use a Bandage. A Bandage also heals a little.

## Companions, the farm and the seasons (1.30.4)
- **Pets fetch:** a tamed animal brings you something from bodies it finds nearby.
- **The hawk:** with Hunting 50, a Falconer's Glove looses a hawk once an hour. It shows you what lives around you for 30 s, and often brings back a hare.
- **Homestead:** after the garden you can build Beehives (honey), an Orchard (apples) and a Livestock Pen (eggs and milk).
  - Their goods go to the steward each day.
  - Farming raises the yield, and collecting the goods trains Farming.
- **Seasons:**
  - The orchard bears double in autumn and nothing in winter.
  - Bees sleep through the winter.
  - Spring flowers bloom only in spring and summer.
  - Fish bite slowly in winter.
  - Bears sleep through the winter.
